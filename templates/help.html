📋 Frequently Asked Questions

🎯 What does each metric mean?
  • Total Customers: All customers in your database
  • High Value: Customers with ₹100+ last purchase (VIPs)
  • High Engagement: Score > 0.8 (likely to open/click)
  • Avg Engagement: Mean score across all customers (0-1)
